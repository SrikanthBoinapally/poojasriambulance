POOJASRI HYDERABAD AREA-WISE WEBSITE PAGES

Contents
- index.html: your original homepage with a new directory linking to 30 locality pages.
- [area-slug]/index.html: one landing page per locality.
- assets/location-pages.css: shared styling for area pages.
- sitemap.xml and robots.txt: templates.

BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Replace YOUR-DOMAIN in every area page canonical URL, sitemap.xml and robots.txt with your real domain (without a trailing slash).
2. Confirm that Poojasri actually accepts enquiries/service requests for every listed area. Remove any areas you do not cover. These pages explicitly say availability must be confirmed; they do not claim local branches.
3. Upload the contents of this folder to the root of your existing GitHub Pages repository, keeping the folder structure. Do not upload the outer ZIP as a single file.
4. Test the homepage and each /area-slug/ URL.
5. Submit sitemap.xml in Google Search Console and request indexing for priority pages. Google decides whether and when pages are indexed or shown.
6. Add unique, genuine local details or service information where available. Do not create doorway pages or copy/paste identical text for many areas.

The original phone number and business identity were retained from your supplied index.html.
